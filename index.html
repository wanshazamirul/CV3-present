Caps and rotation keep the working set fast, like a cache with discipline.

Nothing is deleted

Hot → warm → cold → library. Everything stays findable, forever, by search.

06 · Continuous improvement · 改善

Improvement as a loop.

Observe · daily, deterministic

No AI in the observation pass: counts, corrections, unknowns. Facts only.

Review · weekly, ranked

Trust order decides what lands: corrections > preferences > repetition > one-off flags.

Propose · never overwrite

The system drafts changes; people approve them. Even when the AI is the reviewer, it can't promote itself.

07 · Proof of discipline

The reviewer tried.
The guard refused.

REFUSED

This happened live, in production. The system caught its own AI trying to write where it shouldn't, and stopped it.

AI proposes. Humans decide. It's not a promise in the prompt; it's a wall in the code.

We audit ourselves, and we keep the receipts.
